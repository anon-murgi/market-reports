1. The Underwater Chart — 35 Years of Drawdowns

The market spends 93% of its time BELOW the previous peak. ATH days are the rare exception. This is THE most underrated chart in investing.

2. Drawdown Depth vs Duration

Bubble size = recovery time. COVID: deep + fast. 1994: moderate + painfully slow. 2008: deep + slow (worst combo).

3. Pain Index — Total Investor Suffering

Pain = depth × duration ÷ 100. The 1994 bear market caused MORE cumulative pain than even 2008 (longer duration).

4. Recovery Time by Severity Bracket

Even within the same bucket, variance is huge. A 20% drop can recover in 2 months or 2 years. Never assume "it'll bounce back quick."
