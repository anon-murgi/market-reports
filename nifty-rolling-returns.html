8. Mean Reversion: Past 5Y CAGR vs. Next 5Y CAGR

R = -0.54. High past returns → lower future returns. This is the most reliable signal for deployment timing.
Key Insight: When trailing 5Y CAGR drops below 8%, the next 5 years historically delivered 14–22% CAGR. When above 18%, forward returns compressed to 6–10%. This isn't timing — it's mean reversion math.
