Sector Positioning
Paras is an Indian private defence/space engineering company with two reportable FY26 segments: Optics & Optronic Systems and Defence Engineering. Products include submarine periscopes, EO/IR systems, space optics, hyperspectral systems, defence electronics, electromagnetic protection and heavy engineering. Anti-drone and related RF/microwave products are developed through subsidiaries. The FY26 Annual Report describes design/development and high-entry-barrier applications, but product funnel statements must be separated from contracted backlog.
