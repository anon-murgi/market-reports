Business and defence exposure
LMW operates textile machinery (TMD), machine tools and foundry (MTD+Foundry), and Advanced Technology Centre (ATC). Primary FY26 annual report and Q4/Q1 calls substantiate defence activity in MTD and ATC: management cited defence/aerospace traction in MTD; ATC serves aerospace, space and defence programmes. No standalone defence revenue, profit, orderbook or customer concentration is disclosed. ATC FY26 external customer sales ₹171.29cr are distinct from ₹206.82cr segment revenue including ₹35.53cr allocated other income.
