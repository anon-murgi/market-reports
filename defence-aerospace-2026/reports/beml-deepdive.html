Forward Thesis — Bull / Base / Bear

BULL 20%

Awards convert; margins and utilization improve
  • The ₹5,400 Cr+ NHSRCL Mumbai–Ahmedabad HSR award disclosed on 18 Sep adds material multi-year work, but delivery schedule, margin and revised net order book remain undisclosed.
  • Q1 FY27 Rail & Metro revenue grew 178% YoY and management expects at least 20–25 Bengaluru metro train deliveries in FY27; delivery and acceptance would need to support the high-20s revenue outlook.
  • Management expects FY27 EBITDA margin of at least 13%; if achieved alongside improving working-capital turns, it could support EPS above the ₹70 FY28 analyst base assumption.

BASE 50%

Backlog supports growth; returns and cash remain modest
  • Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹819.62 Cr (+29.3% YoY), but the company reported a ₹27.01 Cr net loss; growth therefore has not yet established profitable full-year execution.
  • The reported 30 Jun order book was ₹16,284 Cr and the September HSR award adds visibility, while management assigned only 30–40% probability to its ₹20,000 Cr FY27 order-intake target.
  • A 30× FY28E P/E on assumed ₹70 EPS gives ₹2,100 versus 25 Sep CMP ₹2,036.5, only ~3.1% implied upside before execution and cash-conversion risks.

BEAR 30%

Execution delays and working capital overwhelm backlog growth
  • If the HSR/metro award schedule, testing or customer acceptance slips, the incremental ₹5,400 Cr+ contract may not translate into near-term revenue or cash.
  • Q1 FY27 loss, 7.7% ROCE and long cash-conversion cycle leave limited resilience if material costs, project provisions or receivables rise while management’s ≥13% EBITDA-margin expectation is missed.
  • At ~95× trailing P/E, a derating toward the archetype 25×–35× range could outweigh order-book growth; the overlay’s bear sensitivity is ₹1,375 (₹55 assumed FY28 EPS × 25×).
