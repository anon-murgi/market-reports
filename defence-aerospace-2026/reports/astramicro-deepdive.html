Company identity and position
Astra Microwave Products Limited (NSE: ASTRAMICRO, BSE: 532493; CIN L29309TG1991PLC013203) designs and manufactures RF/microwave products, radar, electronic warfare, telemetry, space and meteorology systems. It is a private Indian subsystem/system supplier; it is not a platform prime. FY26 annual report details product capability across radar electronics, EW, telemetry, space and meteorology.
Industry structure and competition
Value chain spans DRDO / service requirements, HAL/BEL/BDL primes and integrators, and specialised electronics suppliers. Relevant listed comparisons include Data Patterns, BEL, Bharat Dynamics and select engineering suppliers; scale, program mix, ownership of IP and revenue recognition differ, so multiples are directional only. The defence archetype’s central math is booked order × execution velocity × margin × (1 − working-capital drag).
Demand, policy and cycle
FY27 MoD allocation is ₹7.85 lakh Cr, +15.19% vs FY26 BE; capital outlay is ₹2.19 lakh Cr and ₹1.39 lakh Cr (75% of capital acquisition budget) is earmarked for domestic procurement. This supports demand context, but allocations and AoNs do not guarantee company awards. Source: PIB / Ministry of Defence, 1-Feb-2026.

Domestic defence modernization/self-reliance remains an explicit policy priority; PIB reported on 14-Aug-2026 that FY26 domestic defence production reached ₹1.78 lakh Cr, while FY27 budget had raised defence allocation to ₹7.85 lakh Cr.
