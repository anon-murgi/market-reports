Business and archetype
Cyient DLM is a high-reliability electronics manufacturing services (EMS) provider offering design-led manufacturing/build-to-spec solutions across aerospace, defence, industrial, medical and automotive end markets. It is not a defence prime or platform OEM. Q1 FY27 mix was aerospace 42%, defence 9%, industrial 32%, medical 16%, auto/other 1%; aerospace and defence together 51%.
