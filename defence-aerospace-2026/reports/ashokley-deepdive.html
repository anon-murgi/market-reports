India CV Market — Replacement Cycle in Progress
India's commercial vehicle market is in a GST-triggered replacement cycle. The GST rate rationalization (September 2025) lowered CV prices materially, stimulating fresh demand. Simultaneously, India's MHCV fleet has an average age of 10+ years — well above the 6-8 year replacement norm — creating a structural backlog. MHCV industry volume tracking 20-24% YoY growth (Q3 FY26). Ashok Leyland is India's #2 MHCV player (after Tata Motors) with 30.6% market share, strong in South India (home market advantage) and Defence.
Switch EV — Electric Bus Leadership
Switch Mobility is positioned to capture India's electric bus opportunity — government bus tenders (BEST, DTC, KSRTC equivalents) and school/corporate fleet electrification. EBITDA positive in 9M FY26 is a significant milestone — most EV subsidiaries remain loss-making for years. International bus deliveries (UK previously, now Southeast Asia) validates the product. Switch is a free embedded option in Ashok Leyland CMP — no premium in current valuation for this business.
