Apollo Micro Systems is a private defence electronics and weapon-systems supplier focused on missile, naval, avionics, EW and sensor subsystems, with an emerging systems/OEM ambition. It is much smaller than BEL/HAL/BDL and depends on programme awards and milestone execution. Company claims of presence/content across major indigenous programmes are management representations; distinguish those from awarded contracts.
