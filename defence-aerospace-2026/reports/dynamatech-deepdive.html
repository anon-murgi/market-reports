Sector Positioning
Dynamatic is a multi-segment precision manufacturer. Its aerospace unit supplies complex structures to commercial and defence customers. The company’s official aerospace page names HAL among customers; FY26 Annual Report identifies participation in the L&T/BEL AMCA consortium, and a company case study identifies Tejas LCA front fuselage supply to HAL. Aerospace revenue includes commercial work; current filings do not disclose a separate defence revenue/order-book figure.
Competitive Landscape
The aerospace supply chain is qualification- and certification-intensive, with program-specific design, tooling and quality requirements. Dynamatic competes with Indian precision manufacturers and global suppliers; pure-play listed peers such as Azad Engineering, MTAR Technologies and Paras Defence have different product, customer and margin mixes, so their multiples are not directly comparable. Local industrial/machinery peers also are proxies rather than direct aerospace comps.
