Top Risks
HIGH
Leverage and capex constrain equity conversion
Q1 call net debt was ₹1,900 Cr; management targets at least ₹500 Cr reduction in FY27 while guiding ~₹350 Cr capex plus remaining wheel-JV contribution. FY26 local CFO ₹840 Cr but FCF -₹39 Cr, so cash conversion and capex execution matter as much as EBITDA.
HIGH
Energy and freight recovery remains uncertain
Steel pricing is passed through with a one-quarter lag, but management said energy/gas and shipping increases were still not fully resolved; Q4 described shipping cost +15–20% and transit time +15–20 days. Q1 margin recovery should not be treated as proof of compensation.
MEDIUM
New capacity and rail-wheel approvals may delay revenue
Q1 wheel samples were scheduled for August testing with bulk supply targeted September/October; 80,000 Railway demand plus ~25,000 JV-partner obligation is not the same as revenue realization. Casting ramp and cold-forging PV customer approvals also remain operational gates.
MEDIUM
Inventory discrepancy remains a governance trust overhang
FY26 annual report says the joint fact-finding report quantified book-versus-physical inventory differences of ₹220.52 Cr at Mar-2025 and ₹50.22 Cr at Mar-2024, attributed to erroneous accounting entries/non-recorded rejections; these were fully booked and prior-year comparative restated. FY26 statutory opinion was unmodified and auditors reported controls adequate/effective. This closes the quantified financial adjustment, but does not erase prior process failure or independently prove sustained remediation.
MEDIUM
Exports and customer/platform dependence
Management expects exports >20% growth and ~35% of consolidated FY27 revenue, but did not quantify North American Class 8 demand and the company does not disclose customer concentration by name/share. Tariff, demand, logistics and currency effects are therefore difficult to independently size.
