BEL — The Defence Electronics Integrator
Bharat Electronics is India's primary defence electronics company — the only major listed PSU with capabilities across radars, electronic warfare, avionics, naval systems, C4ISR, and now AI/cybersecurity. BEL's competitive position is unique because electronics is the fastest-growing content layer in modern defence platforms. A modern frigate has 3-5x more electronics value vs a 1990s equivalent. As India's defence platforms upgrade from imported to indigenous, BEL captures the electronics integration contract.
Electronics Content Migration — The Structural Thesis
HSIE's March 2026 thematic ('Electronics Powering India's Defence Upcycle') identifies BEL as the primary beneficiary. Three trends converge: (1) Multi-domain warfare requiring integrated electronics across land/sea/air; (2) Aging asset replacement with electronically superior platforms; (3) MSME ecosystem scaling creating domestic component supply for BEL's manufacturing. The total addressable market for defence electronics is ₹50,000+ Cr/year from FY27-30 as platforms replace 1980s-90s vintage systems.
