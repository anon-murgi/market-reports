Company identity and business map
HBL Engineering Limited (formerly HBL Power Systems Limited), NSE HBLENGINE / BSE 517271, CIN L40109TG1986PLC006745. FY26 Annual Report describes three businesses: Industrial Electronics (Kavach/rail signalling, electric drivetrains, other R&D), Industrial Batteries (lead, NiCd, lithium), and Defence Businesses (defence batteries, electronic fuzes, special defence products). Archetype uses the closest defence/railways template; the template is defence-focused and its PSU governance label is not applicable to HBL, a private issuer.
Defence exposure: evidenced but not fully separated
Defence activity is directly evidenced by the FY26 Annual Report: batteries for aircraft, missiles, torpedoes, submarines, tanks/armoured vehicles and artillery; electronic fuzes for ammunition; and R&D in special defence/underwater electronics. FY26 reportable Defence & Aviation segment sales ₹211.58cr (6.4% of consolidated net revenue) versus ₹227.22cr FY25; segment includes aviation and does not isolate defence revenue. Q1FY27 segment revenue ₹37.93cr and segment result ₹9.10cr, both sharply lower YoY.
Railway electronics and market position
Kavach is not a defence product but a major railway safety revenue source; AR says it represented almost 50% of FY26 sales and should be about the same in FY27. HBL was a pioneer and describes itself as a leading executor; ₹24cr ICF award in July and ₹31.49cr in August 2026 are specific new contracts, but total book and completion performance are not disclosed. AR warns of competitive intensity from FY29.
Sector, peers and macro
W39 defence brief indicates elevated FY27 procurement and contract signing/ordering acceleration, while stressing that AoNs and sector budgets are not company orders and delivery schedules govern revenue. Peer comparison parquet is mismatched; no current comparable valuation table asserted.
