India Capex Cycle: Structural Multi-Year Tailwind
India's government capex is at ₹11.5 lakh crore in FY27 BE (+11.5% YoY), sustaining the infra supercycle that began post-COVID. Power T&D (RDSS: ₹3.04 lakh crore), railways (₹2.65 lakh crore FY27), urban infra (AMRUT 2.0), and JJM 2.0 water (₹8.69 lakh crore total outlay) are the key government spend themes. Private sector capex is accelerating — L&T's domestic private sector OB share jumped from 21% to 39% in FY26, the fastest shift in years, driven by data centres, thermal BTG, semiconductor fabs, and solar EPC. This private capex cycle is more durable than government-driven cycles and benefits L&T disproportionately given its multi-segment EPC capability.
GCC / Middle East: The Wild Card in the Thesis
L&T has ₹3 trillion (~$36bn) of Middle East order book — the single largest non-India EPC exposure. GCC governments have strong balance sheets (low debt, large forex reserves) and continue prioritising national diversification projects (Saudi Vision 2030, UAE Net Zero, Qatar gas expansion). However, the ongoing West Asia conflict has created near-term disruption: logistics + insurance cost inflation, supply chain rerouting, and some order award deferrals. Management estimates $30-50 billion of reconstruction capex over 3 years post-conflict resolution. L&T's modular fabrication model (Chennai/Oman yards) positions it to win a disproportionate share of both reconstruction and new-build GCC capex as normalcy returns. Risk: if the conflict escalates into broader GCC disruption, L&T's Middle East revenue (~53% of group) is materially at risk.
Competitive Positioning: Uncontested at the Top
L&T is India's only truly diversified global EPC conglomerate with a credible presence across hydrocarbon, offshore wind, defence, transportation infra, heavy civil, T&D, and Hi-Tech Manufacturing. No Indian competitor matches this range — NCC, KEC, Kalpataru are sub-segments; Siemens/ABB are equipment/technology businesses not full EPC. The closest global peers are Saipem (Italy), Samsung Engineering, and Tecnicas Reunidas — none with India-scale domestic order books. L&T's scale gives it access to ultra-mega orders (>₹1,000 Cr single tickets) that smaller EPC players cannot bid for. Private sector share now at 39% of domestic OB signals that L&T has penetrated the industrial/manufacturing capex cycle not just government infrastructure.
Capital Goods Peers: Why L&T Trades at a Discount to Siemens/ABB
Siemens India (78x PE), ABB India (89x PE), and Thermax (80x PE) all trade at 2-3x L&T's multiple (31.6x PE). The key reason: product-based businesses with 15-30% EBITDA margins vs L&T's project-based 7.8% PPM margins. Capital goods plays command technology IP and high-repeat annuity revenue; EPC plays are one-off project bookings. L&T is closing this gap by: (a) adding O&M annuity via Hi-Tech Manufacturing; (b) building technology IP in defence/nuclear; (c) seeding data centres and green H2 for recurring revenues. If L31 succeeds, the multiple gap with capital goods narrows. If margin stagnation continues, L&T remains an EPC multiple play capped at 25-35x PE.
