Commercial explosives and proposed Omnia combination
Solar’s core explosives business is tied to mining volumes, infrastructure and quarrying; capacity location, logistics, input-cost pass-through and customer payment terms shape returns. The proposed Omnia acquisition broadens the footprint into BME mining services and upstream nitric acid/ammonium-nitrate capability, while bringing agriculture as a new vertical. Management’s Sep 15 call projected combined FY28 revenue above ₹30,000 Cr and EBITDA ₹6,800–7,000 Cr with synergy; these are forward-looking estimates, not booked results. Omnia’s 14 Sep firm-intention notice values the offer at R21.829bn, R134.50/share, all cash; closing is conditional.

Competitive position and sub-archetype
Solar is a scaled explosives producer with a growing defence energetics and munitions portfolio. Its differentiated opportunity is the combination of commercial explosives, initiating systems, propellants and selected defence products; each defence program still carries qualification, contract and execution risk. Domestic listed explosives peers are substantially smaller; HAL/BEL are defence references but have different platforms, revenue mixes and economics and are not like-for-like valuation comps. The proposed Omnia combination creates an international integration thesis, not evidence yet of realized synergy.
