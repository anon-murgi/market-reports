AXISCADES position / defence exposure
AXISCADES Technologies Limited (NSE: AXISCADES; BSE: 532395) is a Bengaluru-headquartered aerospace, defence, electronics/semiconductor and engineering company. It is not a PSU or defence platform prime: relevant exposure is subsystem design, defence electronics, missile/radar/weapon programme work, Mistral electronics, plus broader aerospace services and new manufacturing. Q1 call cited ₹125 Cr defence revenue and named current subsystem work; total forecast visibility ₹4,557 Cr must not be presented as firm orderbook.
