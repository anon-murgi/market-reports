Paper and packaging market: volume tailwind, realization uncertainty
The issuer’s FY26 annual report estimates India paper demand at about 25m tonnes in FY26, growing around 6%, and packaging board around 8%. These are management estimates. It reports record FY26 paper volume of 8.19 lakh tonnes, while also identifying low-priced imports and wood cost as margin pressure. Imported pulp, recovered fibre, chemicals, energy and freight all affect spread; company-specific unit cost and realized price curves are not disclosed in the reviewed material.
Company positioning and fibre chain
JK Paper combines writing/printing paper, packaging board, packaging conversion, forestry and pulp capabilities. The company describes plantation activity and local wood procurement, and BCTMP commenced production on 30-Jun-2026 to reduce dependence on imported hardwood pulp. Scale, local fibre access and conversion integration are potential advantages; realized cost benefit and project economics remain unproven in disclosed data.
Defence index membership versus actual business exposure
BSE India Defence index official constituent API snapshot (as of 31-Aug-2026) lists JK PAPER LTD, BSE scrip 532162, under index code 155. Issuer filings identify the same legal entity and NSE symbol JKPAPER. Audited segment disclosures place Delopt with other activities in “Others”: ₹185.75cr FY26 revenue (2.63% of group), alongside animal nutrition and housing. This verifies some real defence-related activity but does not quantify defence exposure; JK Paper is not a defence pure-play.
