Business mix and strategic position
MTAR is a high-precision supplier rather than a platform prime. Its current cycle spans civil nuclear reactor components, export-oriented aerospace parts, defence sub-systems, Bloom-linked fuel-cell products, space and emerging oil-and-gas/data-centre manufacturing. Q1 order book ₹5,143 Cr and annual growth target are company-wide; neither should be presented as defence-only backlog or demand. FY26 issuer annual report sector mix: clean energy—fuel cells/hydel/others ₹614.6 Cr (70.16%); Aerospace & Defence ₹103.8 Cr (11.84%); civilian nuclear ₹23.6 Cr (2.69%); products/others ₹134.1 Cr (15.31%). Q1 FY27 deck reports clean-energy order book ₹3,431 Cr out of ₹5,143 Cr total. Only combined Aerospace & Defence revenue is reported; defence-only share is unavailable.
