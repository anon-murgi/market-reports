30. Crash Frequency — "How Often Does the Market Fall X%?"

A 25% crash happens every 4 years. A 50% wipeout every 12 years. These are NOT black swans — they're the base rate.

32. Crash Speed vs Recovery Speed

COVID was the fastest crash AND fastest recovery. 2008 was slow both ways. No two crashes have the same personality.
