Key Insights & Structural Shifts
What this data tells us about India's payments future
Growth

UPI Is Unstoppable — 86% Volume Share

UPI now processes ~217,034 lakh transactions/month (Jan 2026), commanding 86% of retail payment volume. FY26 total: 21,860 crore txns worth ₹284.7L Cr. That's a 12,000x volume growth since FY17. The average ticket size is declining (₹1,305) — a sign of deeper micro-payment penetration.

Structural Shift

Debit Cards Are in Secular Decline

Debit card transaction volumes fell to 1,018 lakh in Jan'26 (vs 16,120 FY avg annualized). UPI has cannibalized debit card PoS use. Bank ATMs are also declining (-3.2% YoY). The future is clear: debit cards are becoming an identity instrument, not a payment instrument.

Growth

Credit Cards Resilient — But Shifting Online

Credit card "Others" (e-commerce + digital) volume at 2,722L now exceeds PoS volume (2,675L) for the first time. Total outstanding: 116.7M cards (+7.1% YoY). Credit cards survive because they serve a different need — credit, rewards, EMI. UPI-on-credit-card is the next frontier.

Infrastructure

UPI QR > Bharat QR — Winner Takes All

UPI QR codes: 7,384 lakh (+15.3% YoY). Bharat QR: 58 lakh (-10% YoY). The divergence is massive — UPI QR is 127x more deployed than Bharat QR. Bharat QR is functionally dead as a merchant acceptance mechanism. PoS terminals growing slowly at 115L (+5.2%).

Structural Shift

Wallets Exploding — 93% YoY Growth in PPIs

PPI wallets outstanding jumped from 8,958L (Jan'25) to 17,268L (Jan'26) — a 93% increase. Driven by fintech wallet proliferation and interoperable wallets via UPI. This is a massive re-architecture of how stored value works in India.

Watch

IMPS Flatlining — UPI Substitution Complete

IMPS volume was 3,740L in Jan'26 — down 1.6% MoM. With UPI handling both P2P and P2M at zero cost, IMPS is losing relevance for retail flows. Its niche: bank-initiated transfers and fallback. Average ticket: ₹17,969 vs UPI's ₹1,305 — different use cases now.
