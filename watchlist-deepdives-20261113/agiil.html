How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • Developer accounting and cash arrive on different timelines. Recognized sales can improve with completion mix while land and construction absorb cash. Presales, collections, contract liabilities and remaining construction costs supply the operating bridge. A 44% reported OPM does not prove new projects can earn the same return.
  • Punjab residential and commercial exposure may benefit from local relationships but concentrates absorption, approvals and land risk. A national property-cycle label cannot support project-level sales or NAV assumptions.

Sector-appropriate normalized earnings / enterprise value analysis

Use project-level NAV/DCF and a normalized earnings cross-check. Account for remaining construction cost, customer advances, debt, taxes and post-QIP dilution. Snapshot P/E 35.6 x and P/B 7.58 x are demanding without a project-level cash bridge; no target is assigned.

  • QIP launch reported by PTI is not completed allotment.
  • Rounded financial tables explain small differences from company-exact growth.
  • Negative FY26 CFO is a historical fact; it does not prove fraud or prevent future collection recovery.

Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • Project NAV should discount expected collections less costs to complete, taxes, finance costs and liabilities. Book value or one-quarter PAT multiplied by four cannot provide that bridge. Land values should not be added on top of an earnings value that already includes the same projects.
  • The bull case needs good absorption and positive cash conversion with productive proceeds deployment. The base case permits profit growth alongside working-capital absorption. The bear case includes slower collections, repeated external funding and dilution. Dated premium P/E and P/B snapshots do not prove intrinsic value.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Regional Punjab developers are the closest operating reference where project data exist. DLF, Godrej Properties and Sobha offer NAV or allocation-framework checks, but geography, project mix, approvals and funding differ. A national developer’s multiple does not automatically justify AGI’s premium.
