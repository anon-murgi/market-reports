How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • Backlog quality depends on mix, execution age, contract price protection, certification and customer funding. A33-month average conversion period gives visibility but does not eliminate project delays, scope changes or cash-collection risk. The growing energy/international mix differs from the domestic infrastructure portfolio.
  • About 54% of backlog was international and 73% of that portfolio was Middle East; multiplying these gives roughly 39% of the total. This is an analyst concentration estimate. It highlights logistics, counterparty and geopolitical exposure even though customer advances can improve near-term working capital.

Execution and project economics

Large orders convert over multiple years, so order growth and earnings growth will not move synchronously. Certification thresholds, advances, provisions and project mix dominate quarterly margins.

Geography and capital allocation

Energy and Middle East execution are central to the book. New AI compute and semiconductor platforms broaden the addressable market but require a separate investment/payback assessment.

Macro sensitivities

Policy rates, inflation, funding conditions and household demand affect these businesses through different channels. The company-specific implications are discussed in the business, valuation and risk sections.

Sector-appropriate normalized earnings / enterprise value analysis

Use SOTP for core EPC/manufacturing, listed technology/finance holdings and other investments, deducting the appropriate parent-level liabilities and holding-company adjustments. Consolidated debt includes financial services and cannot simply be subtracted from an industrial EBITDA value. No price target is assigned without the PAT-attribution and segment-debt bridge.

  • Core PPM margin FY27 target 7.8% is not group OPM.
  • FY27 revenue/order growth guidance 10–12% retained despite H1 strength.
  • NWC below 10% is updated direction; 1.7% is current actual supported by advances.

Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • Start with core PPM earnings under the 7.8% FY27 margin target, then add listed/unlisted interests and deduct appropriate parent-level liabilities and holding-company costs. Newplatforms require their own cash-flow model rather than a narrative premium on the EPC multiple.
  • The bull case requires backlog conversion, margin recovery and successful capital recycling; the base case lets advances normalize and commits full capital to new platforms; the bear case combines weak margins, cash reversal and delayed disposals. Exact fair value is withheld until PAT attribution and group-debt perimeters reconcile.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Cemindia and NCC are narrower project-execution comparisons; BHEL and capital-goods manufacturers have different manufacturing/order cycles. Listed subsidiaries should be benchmarked in their own industries. L&T’s consolidated multiple is not directly comparable with a pure EPC company’s multiple.
