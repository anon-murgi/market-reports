How to use it in the company pages

These are analyst transmission channels rather than company forecasts. For banks, examine asset and liability repricing, liquidity and credit costs. For exporters, separate currency translation from unit volumes and margins. For consumer businesses, test volume, affordability and input-price pass-through. For capital-intensive businesses, focus on funding costs and cash generated by new assets.

Company calls and subsequent filings are required to establish the size and timing of each effect. The company evidence ledgers identify where the current review can and cannot support a conclusion.
