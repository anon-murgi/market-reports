How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • Exports, premium domestic motorcycles and electric mobility are separate growth engines. Q2 exports of 7.8 lakh units and 23% Chetak share support scale, but regional demand, foreign-currency availability and capacity/service execution are different constraints.
  • The central EV nuance is product mix. Management calls the combined EV business, including three-wheelers, margin-accretive; the CFO separately says growing electric two-wheelers still dilute overall margin. Treating the combined statement as proof of Chetak margin accretion would overstate unit economics.

Reverse valuation and cash-earnings constraints; no unsupported point target

The approximately 23× price/available-group-EPS ratio appears more modest than many watchlist names, but the earnings perimeter is changing. Motorcycles, exports, Chetak, BACL and KTM need appropriate economic measures and capital claims. Consolidated P/E alone cannot establish whether the core franchise is cheap.

  • At the 12 November adjusted close of ₹10,945.00, the available four-quarter EPS sum is ₹469.85, implying approximately 23.3× earnings and a 4.29% earnings yield. This is a price/available-EPS calculation, not a forecast or a fully normalized multiple.
  • A 20% contraction in the earnings multiple would require 25% cumulative EPS growth merely to keep the share price unchanged; dividends are excluded. This is algebraic sensitivity, not a prediction of the exit multiple.
  • A genuine SOTP requires core cash flow, finance-company book and credit costs, overseas ownership, debt/guarantees and restructuring adjustments. Unexplained terminal multiples do not replace this work.

Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • About 23× available consolidated EPS mixes manufacturing, financing and an overseas turnaround. A sector P/E on blended earnings risks double-counting value or missing funding claims.
  • A SOTP should value core cash, BACL risk-adjusted book returns and KTM normalized operating cash separately, then account for attributable debt, guarantees and future capital. Until that bridge exists, a precise target grid would overstate confidence.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Domestic motorcycle peers help compare premium share, volume, realizations and core margins. Electric-scooter peers help compare distribution and service, but their valuation does not establish Bajaj’s EV value. Financing and KTM require separate comparables.
  • Exports diversify demand, but regional recovery and incentives can make a current run-rate appear more permanent than it is. Compare through-cycle volume and cash generation rather than assuming every future quarter repeats the current export performance.
