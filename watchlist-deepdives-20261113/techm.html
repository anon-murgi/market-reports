How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • The turnaround depends on winning and expanding multi-year engagements while preserving the cash return on delivery. Total CC growth of 7.2% includes organicCC7.0%; reported-dollar 6.8% and INR15.6% are different currency lenses. Management’s large-client evidence is useful: >$20 m accounts grew 11.7% YoY and 3.1% QoQ, rather than a supposed 11.7% attrition statistic.
  • The $1.023 billion new-deal TCV and $4.270 billion LTM wins support pipeline conversion, but contract value is not revenue and has no universal annual conversion factor. Rebadging and transition costs can front-load people and working capital before steady-state collections.

Forward scenarios · analyst interpretation

BULL
  • Constant-currency growth accelerates as large-deals ramp across verticals.
  • EBIT margin reaches 15% FY27 while AI and delivery efficiency creates further headroom.
  • DSO falls and full-year FCF/PAT reaches 90–100%.
BASE
  • Turnaround delivers moderate growth and approximately 15% margin with normal quarterly volatility.
  • Some efficiency gains are reinvested in growth and talent.
  • Valuation limits upside unless earnings grow consistently and cash conversion improves.
BEAR
  • Deal ramps consume working capital and DSO remains stretched.
  • AI price pass-through and wage costs offset delivery savings.
  • Client budgets weaken and premium earnings multiple contracts.

Turnaround versus structural growth

Cost repair and utilisation can lift earnings before industry demand fully recovers. Track constant-currency growth, large-deal conversion, pricing and client concentration in addition to margins.

AI economics

AI can improve delivery productivity and expand scope, while also passing productivity benefits to clients. Count commercial cash flows rather than partnership announcements or AI labels.

Macro read-through

Global client budgets and telecom spending remain cyclical; rupee movement can help reported INR earnings without proving better USD unit growth.

Macro sensitivities

Policy rates, inflation, funding conditions and household demand affect these businesses through different channels. The company-specific implications are discussed in the business, valuation and risk sections.
