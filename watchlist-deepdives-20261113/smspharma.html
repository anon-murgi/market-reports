Investment thesis and business cycles

Primary source

How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • Ibuprofen is already operating near current capacity, so the next growth step is no longer just recovering utilisation. It requires the enlarged 9,600-tonne configuration to become operational, customer demand to absorb the output and price realisation to remain adequate. Management itself describes commodity ibuprofen prices as cyclical; backward integration improves the cost position but does not eliminate pricing cycles.
  • The 49% high-value API mix and 6–8 prospective FY28 launches diversify the portfolio, but filings, launch timing and mature utilisation are separate stages. The investment question is how much incremental gross profit the new products generate after regulatory and ramp costs, rather than multiplying the current gross margin by all announced capacity.

Reverse valuation and cash-earnings constraints; no unsupported point target

The approximately 42× price/available-TTM-EPS ratio already requires several years of execution from the API base. Backward integration can support earnings, but the cash needed for ₹120 crore remaining capex, a 120–150 day working-capital cycle and a pre-commercial peptide platform reduces the distributable value of near-term accounting profits.

  • At the 12 November adjusted close of ₹523.85, the available four-quarter EPS sum is ₹12.49, implying approximately 41.9× earnings and a 2.38% earnings yield. This is a price/available-EPS calculation, not a forecast or a fully normalized multiple.
  • A 20% contraction in the earnings multiple would require 25% cumulative EPS growth merely to keep the share price unchanged; dividends are excluded. This is algebraic sensitivity, not a prediction of the exit multiple.
  • A separate peptide option value is withheld until product qualification, customer commitments, required capital and cash contribution are known.

Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • At about 42× the available trailing EPS, a substantial part of the outcome depends on earnings growing into the price. A 20% decline in the multiple consumes 25% cumulative EPS growth before any capital appreciation. The valuation therefore needs evidence on cash conversion as well as the FY27 growth/margin outperformance.
  • No point fair value is assigned from a peptide addressable market or a generic 40% industry margin. A defensible model would separate the existing API cash engine from product-specific probabilities, launch windows, ramp capex and customer qualification for the new platform.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Compare the ibuprofen and backward-integration economics with established API manufacturers, while using integrated CDMO businesses only as capability benchmarks. SMS has not yet demonstrated the qualified customer pipeline that would justify importing a mature CDMO multiple.
  • A higher-value API mix may deserve a different valuation from a commodity API producer only if incremental margins persist after the next price cycle and cash returns cover the enlarged asset base. The current evidence supports a capability build, not a completed peer-group migration.
