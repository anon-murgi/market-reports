Earnings-basis reconciliation required

The source snapshot implies EPS of about ₹71.75, while the stored trailing-four-quarter EPS sum is ₹51.43. These earnings bases differ materially; the source P/E is retained as dated context and is not a reconciled current or normalised multiple.

Reverse valuation and cash-earnings constraints; no unsupported point target

The current-price/available-TTM-EPS ratio is about 51.7×, materially above the dated 36.1× snapshot. Licensing base effects, exceptional charges and settlement cash mean neither ratio represents clean recurring earnings. Value should separate commercial operations, appropriately scoped net cash and risk-adjusted innovation after funding needs.

  • At the 12 November adjusted close of ₹2,659.80, the available four-quarter EPS sum is ₹51.43, implying approximately 51.7× earnings and a 1.93% earnings yield. This is a price/available-EPS calculation, not a forecast or a fully normalized multiple.
  • A 20% contraction in the earnings multiple would require 25% cumulative EPS growth merely to keep the share price unchanged; dividends are excluded. This is algebraic sensitivity, not a prediction of the exit multiple.
  • No absolute innovation value is assigned from launch counts, early clinical results or an unfunded market opportunity. A recurring-earnings and settlement-cash bridge is needed first.

Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • The approximately 51.7× current-price/available-EPS ratio and 36.1× snapshot multiple need an earnings/timing bridge; choosing the lower figure would not establish cheapness. Normalized commercial earnings should exclude both non-recurring gains and genuine recurring innovation costs appropriately.
  • A model should value core after-tax cash, deduct ongoing innovation and legal cash needs, then add only probability-weighted pipeline value with ownership and timing explicit. A precise point value would overstate confidence before licensing, exceptional and cash effects are reconciled.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Domestic branded and global-generic peers help assess commercial operations, but licensing and innovation spending distort direct P/E comparisons. A biotech or oncology multiple cannot be applied to the whole group because early assets have uncertain cash and funding profiles.
  • Goa VAI is confirmed in official MDA. It is not a governance clearance or proof that every site has the same status. Compare product economics, regulatory reliability and cash after legal/R&D spending rather than only EBITDA.
