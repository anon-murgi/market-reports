Investment thesis and business cycles

Monetisation is broadening beyond orders

The average client-funding book reached ₹7,450 crore, up 40.5% YoY, while the platform approached 40 million clients. Financing and distribution can reduce the dependence on transaction activity, but funding income also makes credit discipline, liquidity and borrowing costs central to earnings quality.

September 2026 local financials and latest earnings-call transcript

Margins are resilient after normalisation

Management reported consolidated operating margin of 45.1% and standalone margin of 48.9%. Adjusted for IPL seasonality and ESOP reversals, consolidated margin was 43.8% versus 43.6% in Q1; most of the sequential headline improvement is therefore an expense-comparison effect.

September 2026 local financials and latest earnings-call transcript

Recovery can occur without a return to peak speculation

September average daily orders recovered to 5.8 million, 12% above August, and quarterly derivative retail share was 22.1%. Improving client engagement is useful evidence, but the business remains exposed to trading rules and client activity rather than a contractual annuity.

September 2026 local financials and latest earnings-call transcript

How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • The economic engine combines transaction monetisation, interest on client funding and distribution. Gross brokerage was about 55% of revenue versus nearly 60% a year earlier, while interest income reached ₹510 crore. Diversification therefore lowers dependence on pure order counts but adds funding capital, collateral and liquidity requirements.
  • The customer funnel matters more than the 40-million registration headline: acquisition must lead to activation, funded engagement and repeat monetisation. A rebound in September orders is encouraging, but annualising one month would ignore regulatory resets and market sensitivity. Wealth AUM also needs a fee-earning and retention bridge before being valued as mature recurring revenue.

Forward scenarios · analyst interpretation

BULL
  • Client funding and distribution sustain double-digit growth while order engagement recovers.
  • Normalised consolidated margins remain in the low-to-mid 40 s despite investment in wealth and AMC.
  • New products raise revenue per client without an equivalent rise in acquisition cost.
BASE
  • Revenue grows with the client franchise; normalised margin is steadier than reported quarterly swings.
  • Funding costs absorb part of the financing-book expansion.
  • A premium multiple persists only if recurring monetisation becomes a larger earnings contributor.
BEAR
  • Further trading restrictions or risk-off markets reduce orders and active clients.
  • Funding-book growth causes credit or liquidity losses that overwhelm brokerage cost savings.
  • A lower earnings multiple offsets otherwise reasonable business growth.

Business model and competition

Angel One is a broker and distributor, not an exchange. Client acquisition, active-client conversion, funding balances and monetisation per active client matter more than exchange network-effect multiples. Its platform competes for the same household wallet as other discount brokers, banks and wealth platforms.

Regulation changes the earnings mix

The latest call describes a regulatory drag on market volumes and an adapting client base. The useful test is whether recurring funding and distribution income can offset lower orders without excessive risk or acquisition spending.

Macro read-through

Higher policy rates can increase borrowing costs for the funding book; strong equity markets support activity but cannot be assumed throughout a valuation horizon. Stress both lower orders and higher funding costs together.

Macro sensitivities

Policy rates, inflation, funding conditions and household demand affect these businesses through different channels. The company-specific implications are discussed in the business, valuation and risk sections.

  • FY26 local CFO of − ₹4,142 crore should be reconciled with expansion of financing assets and client/funding liabilities before drawing an industrial-FCF conclusion. Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • At the clean close of ₹341 and local trailing EPS of about ₹12.67, the descriptive P/E is approximately 26.9 x, versus the separately dated snapshot’s 27.6 x. This does not certify that all trailing earnings are normalized or split-consistent.
  • As an analyst stress test, 12% earnings growth combined with compression from 26.9 x to 22 x would produce a price factor of roughly 0.92 before dividends. This explains why a good business year need not produce a positive stock return. The 22 x input is a stress assumption, not an estimated fair multiple; the previous arbitrary target-price table has been removed.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Motilal Oswal and Nuvama offer comparisons for wealth/asset-management economics, while retail-focused brokers offer transaction-platform comparisons. Their revenue mix, proprietary investments, funding book and distribution networks differ, so a blended sector P/E is not enough. Angel must demonstrate that the incremental distribution/wealth streams earn recurring fees without disproportionate customer-acquisition or capital costs.
