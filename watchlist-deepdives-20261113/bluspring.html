Investment thesis and business cycles

Mix shift can improve economics

Smart Infra, Energy and Engineering reported a 10.9% EBITDA margin, versus 4.7% in facilities and food services and 3.2% in security. A higher contribution from industrial services can lift group profitability, but integration and ramp-up remain important risks.

September 2026 local financials and latest earnings-call transcript

Organic and inorganic growth must be separated

The call reports 49% YoY revenue growth excluding foundit, versus 16% growth in the existing business. Acquisitions explain much of the gap; total growth should not be treated as fully organic or free of capital requirements.

September 2026 local financials and latest earnings-call transcript

Excluding foundit does not eliminate the cost

The call emphasises PAT of ₹32 crore and EBITDA of ₹67 crore excluding foundit. Local consolidated PAT is ₹13 crore and operating profit ₹54 crore. Foundit losses and reporting-basis differences remain part of the valuation until the business is sold, closed or turned around.

September 2026 local financials and latest earnings-call transcript

How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • The strategy is moving toward energy/industrial services, where current EBITDA margin 10.9% exceeds facilities/food 4.7% and security 3.2%. Better mix can create value, but contract mobilisation, wage pass-through, collection and safety execution still determine cash returns.
  • Revenue growth 49% excluding foundit is materially acquisition-assisted: existing businesses grew 16%. LSG consolidated only from August, making future annualisation meaningful but not free. The investor needs acquired-cohort earnings, integration costs and cash returns on consideration, rather than treating the entire growth rate as organic.

  • Industrial services mix and LSG integration lift margins without collection slippage.
  • Foundit losses fall and consolidated profit converges with core performance.

  • Working capital and foundit funding consume a portion of core cash generation.
  • Valuation requires normalised consolidated earnings rather than best-segment multiples.
BEAR
  • Acquisitions fail to integrate or higher margin contracts demand larger working capital.
  • Foundit losses and borrowing costs offset core EBITDA growth.
  • Low contract margins leave equity profits sensitive to wages, collections and interest.

Contract service economics

Facilities, security, engineering and food services differ in margin, labour intensity and working capital. Revenue growth without contract-level returns or collections is not value creation.

Acquisition strategy

STEAG and LSG bring industrial and aviation-food capabilities. LSG consolidated from August, so future comparisons must separate new scope from organic growth.

Macro read-through

Infrastructure and industrial investment can expand demand, but wage inflation, financing costs and customer payment delays can severely affect thin-margin contracts.

Macro sensitivities

Policy rates, inflation, funding conditions and household demand affect these businesses through different channels. The company-specific implications are discussed in the business, valuation and risk sections.
