Research scope and evidence

How to read this library.

32 company reviews with quarterly financial history, business economics, cash conversion, valuation logic, operating KPIs, catalysts and source-linked material-claim checks.

Evidence review

The ledger contains 419 material claim records: 335 supported by a cited source, 35 reporting-definition differences, 28 corrected records, five analyst inferences and 16 unresolved records. All 31 management quotations were matched to the retained text associated with the cited document. Supporting a statement means the source says it on its stated basis; it does not guarantee forecast delivery or certify company accounts.

Each claim links to an official document and gives a page or text-line locator. The public financial downloads contain company figures used in the report. Private portfolio records, full local input packs, operational logs and archived drafts are excluded from this publication.

Financial definitions

Local statement revenue, operating profit and total PAT are kept distinct from management-adjusted EBITDA, standalone results and profit attributable to owners. Differences are resolved where the evidence allows, and remain flagged otherwise. Snapshot dates and price dates are displayed. Valuation sensitivities and monitoring thresholds are analyst assumptions, not company promises.

Current transcripts are reviewed for 28 companies. UTTAMSUGAR, ORIENTHOT, AGIIL and 63MOONS retain explicit current-call gaps; available official results or corporate disclosures support their other analysis. This is a material-claim research review, not a statutory audit or a complete multi-quarter forensic review.

Charts and notes

Charts use the maintained adjusted local price series and the shared TradingView Lightweight Charts library. Final price dates are shown. Notes are stored only in your browser; there is no server submission or account storage.
