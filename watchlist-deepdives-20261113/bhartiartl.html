How the business earns: economics and competitive position

  • India mobile contributes the largest revenue stream, while Africa, homes/B2 B and passive infrastructure add different currencies, growth rates and capital needs. A single consolidated P/E hides these variations and the economic claims of minority shareholders.
  • ARPU rose from ₹264 to ₹271, but one extra day helped. Postpaid net adds of 1.2 million and smartphone upgrades support premiumisation, while pricing, mix and calendar effects must be separated. A broad tariff hike should not be assumed simply because it would improve the model.

Forward scenarios · analyst interpretation

BULL
  • Premiumisation and rational pricing lift ARPU while postpaid and homes gain share.
  • Capex intensity moderates and debt falls, expanding cash available to shareholders.
  • Data-centres and financial services earn returns above cost of capital without overextending the group.
BASE
  • Mobile and Africa growth continue but new growth bets consume a portion of the cash surplus.
  • ARPU rises gradually with customer mix; tariff-hike timing remains uncertain.
  • Cash generation improves without assigning the highest possible valuation to every adjacent business.
BEAR
  • Competitive or regulatory pressure delays tariff repair.
  • Aggressive reinvestment or credit losses consume expected cash returns.
  • Africa currency or macro shocks reduce consolidated equity cash flows.

Segment-specific valuation

India wireless, Africa, towers, homes, enterprise, data centres and finance have different growth, risk and capital needs. A consolidated P/E does not capture these differences adequately.

Durable pricing versus usage growth

Postpaid mix, smartphone upgrades, convergence and tariff structure are separate levers. Rising data consumption does not automatically translate into higher revenue per GB.

Macro read-through

Africa currency and inflation risk, Indian funding costs and imported network capex can offset operating leverage. Lower leverage helps but does not remove exposure.

Macro sensitivities

Policy rates, inflation, funding conditions and household demand affect these businesses through different channels. The company-specific implications are discussed in the business, valuation and risk sections.

Segment SOTP using EV/EBITDA after leases and DCF; no single consolidated P/E target

The quality case is clearer than the valuation margin of safety. Snapshot P/E 39.4× is not a complete telecom valuation. Segment forecasts, lease liabilities, minorities and net debt must be reconciled before an exact equity value.

  • India and Africa require different discount rates and multiples.
  • Subtract net debt, lease obligations and minorities consistently with the chosen EBITDA definition.
  • Do not add listed subsidiary value and their earnings again in the core multiple.
  • No unscheduled tariff hike or undisclosed financial-services valuation is assumed.

Valuation logic and what the current price requires

  • A defensible SOTP values India telecom, Africa, passive infrastructure, data centres and financial services using their own operating bases and ownership. Lease obligations must be consistent with EBITDAaL; subtracting leases again after fully allowing for lease cash can double-count the economic burden.
  • No point target is asserted because segment forecast, minorities and net debt are not fully bridged. Valuation discipline means credit for existing contracted cash generation, and explicitly risk-weighted assumptions for growth options, rather than assigning a generic technology multiple to financial-services or AI ambitions.

Which peers are economically comparable

  • Reliance Jio is the key operating competitor, but its business is embedded in an unlisted/complex group perimeter; Reliance’s entire P/E is not Jio’s multiple. Vodafone Idea differs materially in leverage, capital needs and network position. Bharti Hexacom is useful for focused regional wireless economics, but its ₹264 ARPU and leverage cannot be substituted for Airtel India’s figures.
