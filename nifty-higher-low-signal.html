19. Methodology

What we're testing: After a drawdown ≥15%, the market hits a trough (T1). It bounces, then pulls back to T2.
Higher Low Signal: T2 > T1 (higher low on weekly closes) AND T2 is within 10% of T1.
Question: When this signal fires, does T1 hold? What are forward returns from T2?
Dataset: 15 qualifying drawdown episodes over 35 years. 6 produced valid higher low signals.

21. Success Rate Summary

Interpretation: The higher low signal has a 83.3% success rate at 3 months — meaning in 83% of cases, the T1 low was never broken in the next 3 months. At 1 year, the rate drops to 66.7%. The signal is useful but NOT infallible — always combine with other indicators (volume, breadth, PE). A higher low alone is a necessary but not sufficient condition for a confirmed bottom.
