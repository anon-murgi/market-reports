15. Growth Curves: SIP vs. Realistic Timer

Both strategies invest Rs 10K/month. The timer exits near peaks (-5% from top), re-enters near troughs (+5% from bottom), and pays 12.5% LTCG on each exit. Tax is gone FOREVER — it never compounds again.
The Tax Drag Reality: The timer paid ₹70.1L in taxes over the period. That money is GONE — it never compounds again. If that ₹70.1L had stayed invested for the remaining years at 12% CAGR, it would have grown to multiples of itself. The real cost of timing isn't the tax rate — it's the lost compounding on the tax paid.
