v2 Audit: 7 Issues Found & Fixed

1
Focused Funds Were Scattered Across Wrong Categories
HIGH

SEBI classifies "Focused" as its own category, but RupeeVest maps them by mandate: EQ-FLEX, EQ-MLC, or EQ-LC. Our v1 categorizer used the RV code BEFORE checking the fund name, so 28 focused funds were absorbed into Flexi Cap, Multi Cap, and Large Cap — competing against 50-80 stock portfolios with a 25-30 stock portfolio.

Before
Focused category: 0 funds. HDFC Focused scored in Multi Cap, ICICI Pru Focused in Large Cap. Concentration Score of 100 (sweet spot for 30 stocks) gave them unfair advantage in 60-stock categories.
After
Focused category: 29 funds. Name keyword "focused" now takes priority over RV category code. All focused funds compete against each other — fair normalization.
2
Contra Category Had Only 3 Funds — Meaningless Normalization
HIGH

With min-max normalization on 3 data points, Kotak Contra automatically scored ~93 (rank #3 overall!) while SBI Contra scored ~8. The spread is mathematically meaningless. Contra's raw P2P of -3.2% was worse than many Large & Mid Cap funds, yet scored higher.

Before
Contra: 3 funds. Kotak Contra score: 92.7 (#3 overall). Score range: 8.4 – 92.7.
After
Value/Contra: 25 funds. SEBI treats them as close cousins. Meaningful normalization. Score range: 36.6 – 79.5.
3
65 Funds Used Regular Plan Expense Ratios Instead of Direct
MEDIUM

Trendlyne has Direct plans (expense ~0.3-1.0%). RupeeVest has Regular plans (expense ~1.5-2.5%). When Trendlyne expense was missing, we fell back to RupeeVest — penalizing 65 funds (esp. ELSS) by 1.15% on average. This made their expense score artificially bad.

Before
ELSS top funds showing 1.57-1.92% expense. Penalized vs 0.38-0.70% Direct plan funds from Trendlyne.
After
RV fallback expense adjusted by -1.15% (median Direct-Regular gap). ELSS top funds now show 0.42-0.67%. Floor at 0.05% prevents negative values.

HIGH

After
